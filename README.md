# Remix of SavvySwim

Create a high-converting, mobile-first landing page for a car hail damage repair company called Perfect Hammer Hail Team.

The goal is to generate phone calls and insurance inspection requests from homeowners after a hailstorm.

The design must feel premium, trustworthy, insurance-approved, and calm (not salesy or aggressive).

🎨 DESIGN STYLE

Clean modern automotive + insurance industry look

White space, structured sections, strong hierarchy

Colors: black, white, yellow accents

Professional typography (bold headlines, clean body text)

Mobile optimized first

Trust-focused (not hype-focused)
