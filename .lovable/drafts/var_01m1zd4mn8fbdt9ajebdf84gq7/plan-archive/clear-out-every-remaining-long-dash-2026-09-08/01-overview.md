# Clear out every remaining long dash

The visitor-facing pages are already clean. There are still 206 long dashes left across the rest of the project: the staff-only screens, the alert messages the system sends your team, the notes developers leave in the files, and the separate CRM launch page.

This pass removes all of them, everywhere, so no long dash is left anywhere on the site or in anything it sends.

Each one gets replaced by punctuation that keeps the sentence reading naturally:

- a full stop when a new sentence starts after it
- a comma when the dash was just a pause
- a colon when the dash introduced a value, like "Leads: 42"
- a vertical bar in list headings and screen titles
- nothing at all when it was only a spacer

The only exception is "Dallas-Fort Worth", where the short dash is part of the region's real name and stays.
