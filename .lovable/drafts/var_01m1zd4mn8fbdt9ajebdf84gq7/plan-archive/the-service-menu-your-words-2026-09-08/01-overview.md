# The service menu, your words

The services page swaps its eight photo cards for the plain menu you wrote — one clear list of everything Savvy Swim does.

## What the section says

Heading: **WHAT DO WE OFFER?**
Under it, in script/italic: *I thought you'd never ask!*
Group label: **POOL CARE**

Then the fourteen items, in your order:

Weekly maintenance plans · Filter clean · Green pool recovery · Algae removal · Tile & coping descaling · Acid washing · Diagnostic service call · Equipment repair & replacement · Leak detection · Inspections · Salt systems · Automation service · Pool resurfacing & remodeling

(That is thirteen named services plus the "Pool care" group they sit under.)

## How it behaves

Each line is a button. Tapping one starts a quote request with that service already filled in, exactly like the old cards did. Numbered, in caps, with a thin rule between each — reads like a printed menu, no photos.

## What stays and what goes

Goes: the eight photo cards, their paragraphs and "what's included" checklists, and the "Six / Six" counter.

Stays: everything else on the page — the intro, the numbers strip, how a visit happens, the Swim Club membership section and its questions, the service-area map, and the call/text bar.
