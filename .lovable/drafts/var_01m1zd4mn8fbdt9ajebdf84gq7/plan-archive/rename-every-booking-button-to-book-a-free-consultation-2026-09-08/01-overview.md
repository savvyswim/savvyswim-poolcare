# Rename every booking button to "Book a free consultation"

Every button on the public site that currently says some version of "free inspection" will read **Book a free consultation** instead. Nothing else changes: the buttons go to the same booking form, and the rest of the page wording stays as it is for now.

Buttons being renamed:

- Home page: the big opening button and the matching button lower down the page
- Top menu bar (the small red button in the header)
- Weekly pool service page: three buttons
- City pages: the booking button
- Our Work page: the booking button
- Schedule page: the form's send button
- The pop-up booking box: its title and send button
