# Take the long dashes out of the site's writing

Right now a lot of the sentences on the site are joined with a long dash: "Pick a time — we'll confirm by phone", "On duty — 24/7", "Pool Cleaning Plano TX — Weekly Service & Repair". This removes that dash everywhere a visitor can read it and repunctuates the sentence so it still reads correctly.

## How each one gets rewritten

The dash is never just deleted — that would leave sentences running together. Each one becomes the punctuation that fits:

- **Two full thoughts** get a period: "Pick a time. We'll confirm by phone or email within one business day."
- **A trailing add-on** gets a comma: "Weekly pool cleaning for Plano homeowners, chemistry balanced, baskets emptied."
- **A label in front of a value** gets a colon or nothing: "On duty: 24/7", "Savvy Swim home".
- **Page titles and browser tab names** switch to the vertical bar already used elsewhere: "Pool Cleaning Plano TX | Weekly Service & Repair | Savvy Swim".
- **Empty table cells** that show a bare dash become blank.

## What this covers

Every visitor-facing page: home, services, weekly pool service, the city pages, our work, schedule, thank-you, leave a review, privacy, terms, the sign-a-contract page, the booking form and pop-up, the header, footer and call button, plus the wording inside the emails and texts the site sends.

## Two things stay as they are

- **"Dallas–Fort Worth"** keeps its dash. That short dash is part of the region's real name, not a sentence break.
- Notes written for me inside the code, and the staff-only admin screens, are not visitor-facing, so they are left alone. Say the word if you want the admin screens cleaned up too.
