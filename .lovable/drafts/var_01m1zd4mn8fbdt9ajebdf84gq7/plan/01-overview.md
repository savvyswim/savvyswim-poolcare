# Five named sections, in caps

The home page keeps its five parts and one-page scroll. Every label — in the top menu and above each section — becomes exactly one of your five names, shown in capitals:

- WHO WE ARE
- WHAT WE OFFER
- HOW IT HAPPENS
- WHY PEOPLE GO SAVVY
- CONNECT WITH US

Two things change today: the opening section gains a WHO WE ARE label it doesn't currently show, and the last section, now called "Contact", becomes CONNECT WITH US. The other three keep their meaning and just switch to capitals.

The larger sentence under each label (for example "Pick a service to see what it costs.") stays as it is, so the page still reads naturally.
