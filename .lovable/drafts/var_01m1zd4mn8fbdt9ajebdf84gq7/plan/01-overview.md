# A simple front page

The home page today runs long: hero, ticker, process, cleaning detail, membership, referrals, gallery, about, stats and contact. The new version keeps five things in order, and everything else moves to a page of its own.

1. **Who we are** — the company name, one short line about what Savvy Swim does, and the two buttons (free inspection, call).
2. **What we offer** — a short grid of offers. Each one is clickable and lands on the page that breaks down what it costs.
3. **How it happens** — the three or four steps of a visit, kept short.
4. **Why people go Savvy** — the customer reviews already on the site, reused word for word.
5. **Contact** — phone, email, hours and the inspection button.

The header menu is trimmed to match those five sections so nothing links to a section that no longer exists.
