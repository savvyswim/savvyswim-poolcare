# Reviews you control, collected on your own site

Today the six customer quotes on the home page are typed into the code, so changing one means a code edit. And the review link you text customers sends them straight to Google — nothing is kept for you.

This adds three things:

1. **A review page on your site** — `savvyswimservices.com/leave-a-review`. A customer picks 1–5 stars, writes a short comment, and adds their first name and city. After they send it, the Google review button still appears, so happy customers can also post there.
2. **A private reviews desk** — a page only you can open, listing every review that comes in. You can approve, hide, edit the wording, fix the name or city, feature it, or add a review yourself (for someone who told you over the phone).
3. **Real reviews on the home page** — the "Why people go Savvy" section shows the approved ones instead of the fixed six. If nothing is approved yet, it keeps showing the current quotes so the page is never empty.

Nothing a customer submits appears publicly until you approve it.
