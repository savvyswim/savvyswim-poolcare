# Survey: name and phone only, address optional with Google lookup

Right now the last step of the survey asks for name, phone, email and address,
and all four are required. This change makes it faster to finish, so fewer
people drop out right at the end.

## What changes on the contact step

- Required: name and phone. Nothing else blocks the button.
- Email: optional, labelled "Email (optional)".
- Address: optional, labelled "Pool address or zip (optional)", and it uses the
  same Google powered address lookup as the quote form, so typing a few
  characters offers real addresses to tap, plus a "Use my current location"
  button.
- The authorization checkbox stays required, since it is how we are allowed to
  call or text them.
- Button copy stays "Claim my free inspection".

Everything before that step, the offer copy and the eight questions, stays
exactly as it is today.
