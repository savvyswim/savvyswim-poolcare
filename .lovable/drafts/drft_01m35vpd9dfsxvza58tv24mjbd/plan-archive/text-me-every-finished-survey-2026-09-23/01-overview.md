# Text me every finished survey

When someone completes the survey and taps the button, a text lands on
817-663-7665 within seconds, on top of the office email that already goes out.

## What the text says

```text
Savvy Swim survey: Maria Lopez
817-555-0134
Plano 75024
Has a company, pays $150-$200, weekly
Ref SS-10482
```

Name, phone, city or zip, a short line of their key answers, and the reference
number, so you can call back straight from the text. Nothing else is needed to
act on it.

## When it fires

- Only for finished surveys, so you are not texted for every page form.
- Once per lead. A repeat submission by the same person inside the existing
  ten minute window is already blocked before it reaches this point.
- If texting is temporarily unavailable, the lead is still saved and emailed;
  the text is never allowed to break a submission.

The number is already used for the site's ops alerts, so no new setup, no new
account, and nothing for you to configure. Say the word later if you also want
a text for the quote and water test forms, that is a small follow-up.
