# Survey on one page, plus a full marketing permission box

Two changes to the free inspection survey page.

## 1. Everything on one page

Today the survey shows one question at a time with Next, Next, Next, and the
contact fields only appear at the end. Instead, every question and the contact
fields sit on a single scrolling page with one button at the bottom.

- All 8 questions stacked in order, each with its answer choices visible.
- The follow up question about who they use now still appears only when they
  say they have a company, it just opens in place instead of on a new step.
- Contact fields at the bottom: name and phone required, email optional,
  address optional with the address suggestions and map preview kept as is.
- The step counter and Next and Back buttons go away. A slim progress bar can
  stay at the top, filling as questions get answered, so people see how far
  along they are.
- One button: "Claim my free inspection". If something required is missing, the
  page scrolls to the first problem and shows the message under it.
- Answers stay optional to skip except the ones already marked required, so
  nobody gets stuck.

## 2. A stronger permission box

The one checkbox today mixes the reply to this request with everything else.
It becomes two clearly separate boxes:

1. **Required:** permission to reply to this request by phone, text and email,
   including automated messages, with rates, frequency, STOP and HELP wording,
   and links to the Privacy Policy and Terms.
2. **Optional marketing:** separate opt in for promotions, offers, seasonal
   pool reminders and news by text and email, stated as not a condition of
   purchase, with its own STOP and unsubscribe wording.

The second box is genuinely optional and unchecked by default, which is what
keeps the marketing consent valid. Both boxes carry visible links to the
Privacy Policy and Terms, and the exact wording the visitor agreed to is stored
with the lead so there is a record of what they accepted and when.
