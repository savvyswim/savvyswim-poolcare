# Survey page: hide the water test tab, one permission box

Two changes, survey page only.

## 1. Remove the floating "Water test" tab on /survey

The blue side tab already stays hidden on the home page. It keeps showing on
the survey page and competes with the survey itself. It will be hidden on
/survey too, and stay exactly as it is on every other page.

## 2. One permission box instead of two

Today the last step has a required box (reply to my request) and a separate
optional box (marketing). They become a single required box that the visitor
must tick to send the survey, with every legal point kept in it:

- Savvy Swim may contact them by call, text and email about this request,
  including automated or prerecorded messages and appointment updates
- Message and data rates may apply, message frequency varies
- STOP to opt out, HELP for help
- Marketing texts and emails (offers, seasonal reminders, company news), with
  a plain line that ticking is not a condition of any purchase and they can
  reply STOP or use the unsubscribe link at any time
- Links to Privacy Policy and Terms, both opening the existing pages

One note before it is built: in the United States a marketing permission is
supposed to be separate and optional, so folding it into the required box is a
risk if a visitor later complains. The plan still does what you asked, keeps
the "not a condition of purchase" and opt-out wording inside the box, and the
lead still records the full text that was shown.
