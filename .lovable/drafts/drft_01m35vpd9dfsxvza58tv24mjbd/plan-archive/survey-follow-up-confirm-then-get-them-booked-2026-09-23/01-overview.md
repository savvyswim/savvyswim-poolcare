# Survey follow up: confirm, then get them booked

Today a survey lead gets one confirmation email and then nothing. This adds a
booking link to that confirmation and three polite nudges afterwards, so people
who never picked a date are pulled back to the booking page.

## What the lead gets

1. Right away: the confirmation they already get, now with a big "Pick my
   inspection time" button that opens the booking page with their name, phone
   and address already filled in. They only choose a date.
2. Day 1, day 3, day 7 after the survey, only while they still have not booked:
   a short reminder with the same button. Each one has its own wording, the last
   one closes with the offer and the phone number.
3. Every nudge also goes out as a text message when they ticked the permission
   box for texts, which matters because the survey lets people skip email. If
   there is no usable email and no text permission, nothing is sent and the lead
   simply stays in the office list.
4. As soon as the lead is booked, converted, or marked not interested, the
   remaining nudges stop. Each nudge is recorded so a lead never gets the same
   one twice, even if the job runs again.

Texts go only to people who gave permission, carry the STOP wording, and a STOP
reply already stops everything today.

## What you will see

The office alert and the CRM handoff are unchanged. In the lead dashboard each
request shows which follow ups went out and whether they landed, using the same
activity list already recorded per lead.
