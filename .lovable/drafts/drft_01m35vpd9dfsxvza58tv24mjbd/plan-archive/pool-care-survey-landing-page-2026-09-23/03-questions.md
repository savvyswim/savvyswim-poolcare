## The questions

1. Do you currently have a pool service company, or do you maintain your pool
   yourself? Have a company / Do it myself / Mix of both
2. If you use a company, who do you currently use? (open text, optional)
3. How much do you pay now per month? Under $100 / $100 to $150 / $150 to $200 /
   $200+ / Not sure
4. How often does your current service come? Weekly / Every other week / 2 times
   a month / 3 times a month / Not on a regular schedule
5. What matters most to you in a pool company? (pick top 2) Reliability and
   showing up on time / Price / Communication, updates, photos, texts / Water
   quality and consistent chemical balance / Trustworthy techs on your property
   when you are not home / Easy billing, no surprise charges
6. What frustrates you most about pool service, past or current? (open text,
   optional)
7. How often does your filter currently get cleaned? Every 1 to 2 months / Every
   3 months / Not on a schedule / Not sure
8. What would make you switch companies, or start using one? Better price /
   Better communication and app updates / A trial period or discount / Nothing,
   happy with current setup

Then a final step: name, phone, email, address or zip, plus the same contact
authorization checkbox the other forms use, with a "Claim my free inspection"
button.

## How it behaves

- One question per step on phones, with a progress bar and back button. Answers
  carry over if they step back.
- Question 2 only shows when the answer to question 1 is "Have a company" or
  "Mix of both".
- Question 5 allows up to two picks and blocks a third until one is unselected.
- Contact step requires name, a phone or email, and an address or zip; the open
  text questions are always skippable.
- On submit the answers go to the leads pipeline, the visitor is redirected to
  the existing thank you page, and the free inspection confirmation continues to
  reach the office and the CRM as it does today.
- If the submit fails, an inline message keeps their answers on screen and
  offers a retry plus the phone number, so a lead is never lost silently.
