# Meta ad landing page

A single page built for people arriving from a Facebook or Instagram ad. One offer, one action: start the short pool survey and claim a free first inspection.

New address: `savvyswim.com/offer`

## What the visitor sees

1. Headline offer: first pool inspection free, no commitment. Full water test, full system check, written report, and a free first filter clean when they switch.
2. One big button, "Claim my free inspection", going to the survey. A second identical button sits at the bottom of the page.
3. Three short proof points: local DFW routes, real techs on a weekly schedule, clear pricing with no surprise charges.
4. What happens next, in three steps: answer 8 quick questions, we call to set the visit, tech runs the free inspection.
5. Cities we serve, the phone number, and the hours block already used elsewhere on the site.

No top navigation and no extra links out, so the only way forward is the survey or a phone call. That is what keeps an ad page converting.

## Ad tracking

Whatever tags the ad link carries (source, campaign) are handed through to the survey, so a lead that starts here is still recorded as coming from the Meta campaign in the ads report. The page itself is marked as not for Google search results and stays out of the sitemap, since it is an ad destination, not a search page.
