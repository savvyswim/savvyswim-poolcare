# Survey: tap to answer, almost no typing

The survey already lives at savvyswim.com/survey on one page. This change turns
the two write-in questions into tap-to-pick lists, so a visitor can finish the
whole survey with their thumb and only types their name and phone at the end.

## What changes

1. "Who do you currently use?" becomes a list of the common local options:
   Blue Haven, ASP America's Swimming Pool Co, Pool Troopers, a local
   independent tech, a neighbor or handyman, I do not know the name, plus
   "Other" which opens a small box to type the name. Still optional, still only
   shown when they say they have a company.

2. "What frustrates you most about pool service?" becomes a pick-any list:
   missed or skipped visits, green or cloudy water, no communication or updates,
   surprise charges, damage or careless work, hard to reach anyone, nothing
   really. It also has "Other" with a small box for their own words. Optional.

3. Any question with "Other" selected shows one short text box under it. If they
   pick Other and leave it blank, the answer is simply recorded as "Other".

Everything else stays as it is: same questions, same one page, same required
name and phone, optional email and address, both permission boxes, and the same
thank you page after submitting.
