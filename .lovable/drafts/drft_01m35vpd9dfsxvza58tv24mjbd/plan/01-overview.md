# Clean ad page and survey, plus a warmer thank you

Two changes.

## 1. Nothing pops up on the ad page or the survey

On `/offer` and `/survey` the visitor sees only the page: no new customer offer card, no cookie bar, no floating water test tab, no quote box. One offer, one action, nothing in the way.

The rest of the site keeps the offer card and the cookie bar exactly as they are today.

One thing worth knowing: the cookie bar is how visitors accept tracking. With it hidden on these two pages, the Meta ad pixel does not start counting until the person moves onto another page of the site or answers the bar elsewhere. Your own lead records, office email and the ads report are unaffected, since those do not depend on the bar.

## 2. Thank you page signs off with your line

After someone finishes the survey or any request, the thank you page closes with:

"We look forward to serving you. On duty, so you don't have to be."

It sits under the existing next steps, in the brand style, so the last thing they read is your promise.
