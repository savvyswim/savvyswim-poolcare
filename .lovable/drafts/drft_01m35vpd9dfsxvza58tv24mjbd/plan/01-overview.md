# Add a pool size question to the survey

One more selectable question on the survey page, so every lead arrives with the pool size and the office can price the visit without a call back.

## The question

**What size is your pool?**

Options, tap one:

- Small, under 10,000 gallons
- Medium, 10,000 to 20,000 gallons
- Large, 20,000 to 30,000 gallons
- Extra large, over 30,000 gallons
- Spa or hot tub only
- Not sure

"Not sure" is a real answer, so nobody stalls on it. The technician confirms the size at the free inspection.

## Where it sits

It goes near the top, right after the questions about who services the pool now and before the pricing question, since size is what makes the price make sense.

```text
1  Do you have a pool service company
2  Who do you currently use
3  What size is your pool        <- new
4  How much do you pay per month
5  How often do they come
...
```

The answer flows into the lead notes and the office email with the rest of the survey, exactly like the other questions. Nothing else on the page changes: still one page, one submit button, one permission box.
