# Pool care survey landing page

A standalone page at `/survey` that asks eight short questions, captures contact
details, and sends the visitor to the thank you page when they finish. Every
completed survey lands in the leads pipeline exactly like a free inspection
request, with all answers attached.

system, and hand you a complete report. Switch, and your first filter cleaning
