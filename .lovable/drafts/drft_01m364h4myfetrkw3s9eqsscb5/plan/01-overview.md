# Sharper photos, faster site, clean Savvy Swim look

Three things: make every photo look crisp, make pages open and change faster, and make sure nothing on the site hints at anything but Savvy Swim.

## About "14k"

Your photos came from a phone, and the biggest copies we have are roughly 1,200 to 1,900 pixels wide. No tool can add detail that was never in the original, so a true 14K version is not possible from these files. What is possible, and what fixes the soft look you are seeing:

- Serve a sharper copy on high resolution screens, so a photo shown at 600 pixels wide actually downloads a 1,200 pixel file. This is the real cause of the pixely look.
- Raise the quality setting so edges and water stay clean instead of blocky.
- Add a modern format (AVIF) with WebP as backup, which gives better quality at a smaller file size.

If you want genuinely huge images, send me the original files straight off the camera or phone, or new photos, and I will rebuild the set at full size.
