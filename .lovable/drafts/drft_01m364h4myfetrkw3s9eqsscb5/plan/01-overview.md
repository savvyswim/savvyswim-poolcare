# Shorten the pool care survey

Two questions come off the survey page:

- Question 2, "What size is your pool?"
- Question 7, "How often does your filter currently get cleaned?"

Everything else stays exactly as it is. The survey drops from 8 questions to 6, the counter at the top reads "0 of 6 answered", and the remaining questions renumber themselves automatically.

Nothing already collected is deleted. Past survey answers stay attached to the leads that sent them, and the write up that goes to the office and the CRM simply no longer includes those two lines going forward.
