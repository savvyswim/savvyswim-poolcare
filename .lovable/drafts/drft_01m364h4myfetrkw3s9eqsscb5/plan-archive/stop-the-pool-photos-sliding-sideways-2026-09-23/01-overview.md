# Stop the pool photos sliding sideways

On a phone the three pool photos under the Savvy Swim name sit in a strip that slides side to side and runs off the edge of the screen. That goes away.

Instead all three photos sit side by side inside the screen, evenly sized, nothing cut off and nothing to swipe. They get a little shorter so the row stays compact and the text below it comes up sooner.

On larger screens the three photos already sit still in a row, so nothing there changes. After this there is no side to side sliding on the photos at any screen size.

The photos themselves, their order and their quality stay exactly the same.
