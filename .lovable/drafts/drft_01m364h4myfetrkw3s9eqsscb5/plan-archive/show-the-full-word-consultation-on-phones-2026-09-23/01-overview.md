# Show the full word "Consultation" on phones

Right now the red button in the top bar says "Consult" on a phone and only says "Consultation" on bigger screens. It also sits hard against the right edge of the screen.

After this change the button reads "Consultation" on every screen size, sits fully inside the screen with a little breathing room on the right, and stays easy to tap.

To make room on a narrow phone, the phone number next to it shrinks to just the phone icon on the smallest screens, and grows back to the full number as soon as there is space. Tapping the icon still calls the same number.
