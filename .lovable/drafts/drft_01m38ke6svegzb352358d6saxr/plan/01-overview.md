# One Savvy Swim system: website, customer app, office app

## Short answer

Merging is very doable. This site already has much of an office system built in: bookings, calendar, follow-ups, ad reports, schedule visits, customers and visits. The SavvySwim app (savvyswim.app) is separate, and today the website only sends leads to it. My recommendation is to make **this project the one home for everything** and retire the separate app in stages, so nothing breaks while you are working.

About app store apps: I can build both apps so they work like real phone apps (home screen icon, full screen, fast). Putting them in the Apple and Google stores is an extra last step that needs **your own Apple Developer account ($99/year) and Google Play account ($25 once)**. I can prepare everything, but you or a developer submits them. I recommend launching as phone-installable apps first, then adding the stores.

## What you get

**Website** (savvyswim.com): stays exactly as it is for visitors.

**Customer app** (sign-in for pool owners):
- Next visit up top: day, arrival window and technician
- Past visits with service notes, photos and water test results
- Pay invoices online
- Book, reschedule or cancel a visit (Mon to Fri, 8 AM to 6 PM)
- Message the office and see replies

**Office app**, one sign-in with two views:
- Office staff: leads, bookings, calendar, follow-ups, customers, invoices, messages, reports
- Technicians (phone first): today's route, visit checklist, chemistry readings, photos, mark visit done. The customer sees the report right away.
