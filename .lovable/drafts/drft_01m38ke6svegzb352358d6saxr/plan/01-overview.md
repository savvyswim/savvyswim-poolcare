# Lead inbox, customer portal, lead scores and the office test

## 1. Every new lead in the app inbox, with a real email
Already in place from this morning: each new lead emails hi@savvyswim.com and becomes a conversation in the app inbox. Still to add:

## 2. Customer portal, one login per lead
- When a lead books (a visit is scheduled), we create a customer login for them automatically and email a "Set up your account" link. The office can also press **Invite to portal** on the CRM lead or booking page.
- At savvyswim.com/portal the customer sees only their own: booking request and reference, confirmations we sent, upcoming and past visits with arrival window, a "Request a different day" button, and messages to the office.
- The CRM lead and booking pages show "Portal: invited / active" and an **Open their portal view** link for the office.

## 3. Lead score and source
- Each CRM lead gets a **source** (Google, Meta ad, Google Business, direct, city page, booking page, survey, water test, app) and a **score from 0 to 100**.
- Score adds up simple, visible points: gave a real email (+10), agreed to texts (+10), picked a day or time (+20), booking page or survey done (+15), in one of our 12 route cities (+15), weekly service or Swim Club interest (+15), came from a paid ad or Google Business (+5), replied to us (+10). Points drop if we couldn't reach them in 7 days.
- The CRM lead list and the bookings list get Score and Source columns, sort by score, and a Hot (70+) / Warm / Cold label. Each lead shows why it got its score.
